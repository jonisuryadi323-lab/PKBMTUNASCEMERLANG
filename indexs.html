Strategi Membaca Cerdas (Reading Strategies)

6 Langkah MUDAH untuk memahami teks Bahasa Inggris secara efektif

1 PREVIEW

Lihat judul, gambar, dan bentuk paragraf sepintas sebelum mulai membaca secara penuh.

2 PREDICT

Tebak atau perkirakan topik bacaan berdasarkan judul dan kata yang sering muncul.

3 READ

Baca teks dengan teliti. Jangan khawatir jika ada 1-2 kata asing yang belum diketahui.

4 FIND INFO

Cari kata kunci (nama, angka, waktu) untuk menjawab pertanyaan spesifik.

5 UNDERSTAND

Tebak arti kata sulit dengan melihat konteks kata-kata lain di sekitarnya.

6 SUMMARIZE

Simpulkan gagasan utama (main idea) dari setiap paragraf secara ringkas.

Level: A1-A2 CEFR

Siti's Healthy Routine: Work, Study, and Stay Fit

P1 Siti is a 25-year-old student in Paket C. She works at a local bakery in the morning and studies English in the evening. Even though she has a busy schedule, Siti always prioritizes her health and well-being.

P2 Every morning at 5:30 AM, Siti drinks a large glass of water and does light stretching exercises for 15 minutes. She eats a simple, healthy breakfast like oatmeal with fruit or boiled eggs before going to work. "A nutritious breakfast gives me energy for the whole day," says Siti.

P3 At night, after finishing her work and study modules, Siti turns off her smartphone 30 minutes before bedtime. Reading a printed book helps her relax and fall asleep easily. She believes that good sleep, healthy food, and daily exercise are key to manage work and study effectively.

Key Vocabulary (Kosakata Penting):

prioritize (verb): mengutamakan
well-being (noun): kesehatan/kesejahteraan
nutritious (adj): bergizi
energy (noun): tenaga/energi
manage (verb): mengelola / mengatur
schedule (noun): jadwal
